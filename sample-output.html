NTPC REL invites bids for 1 GW solar EPC in Andhra Pradesh

NTPC Renewable Energy Limited (NTPC REL), a wholly owned subsidiary of NTPC Green Energy Limited, has invited bids for the EPC package of a 1 GW ground-mounted solar PV project at Kambadur in Anantapur district, Andhra Pradesh. The project is tentatively planned as two 500 MW units, and bids will be taken under domestic competitive bidding on a single-stage, two-envelope basis, followed by a reverse auction.

• Total Capacity: 1,000 MW (AC), configured as 2 × 500 MW units
• Location: Kambadur, Anantapur district, Andhra Pradesh
• Mandate: Turnkey EPC + 3-year comprehensive O&M from COD
